Assumptions and limits - please read
  • The first row is your starting balance. The next row is the financial year ending 30 June {{ t.first_fy }}, and your age is shown as it will be on that date ({{ s.age }} now, {{ s.age + 1 }} then).
  • Contributions are assumed to arrive evenly through the year, so they earn about half a year of growth in the year they are paid.
  • Growth rates are treated as the return after investment fees and tax on earnings. Real results vary year to year, and the figures are not adjusted for inflation.
  • Employer contributions are calculated on your salary and reduced by the contributions tax rate above. Personal contributions are treated as after-tax money with no further tax taken. Contribution limits (caps) are not checked, so confirm them for your situation.
  • The default employer rate is 13% as you asked. The legislated Super Guarantee has been 12% since 1 July 2025, so check what your employer actually pays.
  • This is a planning estimate, not financial or tax advice.
