Assumptions and limits - please read
  • Gearing is judged on the tax result: rent minus all deductible costs, including loan interest and depreciation. A property can be negatively geared for tax yet still be close to cash-neutral, because depreciation is a deduction you don't pay in cash.
  • Negative gearing law. Reports say legislation passed in June 2026 that stops losses on established homes bought after 12 May 2026 being deducted against salary from 1 July 2027. Losses are carried forward. Homes held before that date, and new builds, keep the current treatment. The ATO guidance is still being finalised, so confirm with a tax agent.
  • Interest is calculated on the whole loan for the year (interest-only). Principal repayments are not deductible.
  • Default costs are typical estimates. Check them against your own agent statements, rates notices and insurance. Water assumes you pay only the fixed charges and the tenant pays usage.
  • Tax uses resident rates with brackets held flat and a 2% Medicare levy. Offsets, HELP debt and the Medicare levy surcharge are ignored. Agent fees are entered as a share of rent collected.
  • This is a planning estimate, not tax advice.
