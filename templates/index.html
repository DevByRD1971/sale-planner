Assumptions and limits - please read
  • Proposed law. The 2026-27 Federal Budget announced replacing the 50% CGT discount with CPI indexation and a 30% minimum tax on gains made from 1 July 2027. These changes were still proposals when this was built. Check the final legislation.
  • Gains up to 1 July 2027 keep the 50% discount; only growth after that date is indexed and subject to the minimum tax. Your value on 1 July 2027 is estimated from today's price using the scenario growth rate. A real valuation will be needed for your tax return.
  • Treated as an investment property owned by an Australian tax resident, held over 12 months, with no main residence exemption.
  • Selling costs (agent fees and renovation/cleaning) are deducted from the gain made after 1 July 2027. Renovation spend is not separately indexed.
  • 2026-27 resident tax rates are used (15% second bracket, 14% for sales from 2028), with the brackets held flat and a 2% Medicare levy added. Offsets and the Medicare levy surcharge are ignored.
  • Property growth rates are planning assumptions you can change, not predictions.
  • This is a planning estimate, not tax or financial advice. Please confirm figures with a registered tax agent before you decide.
